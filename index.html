What We Do

At Premier Chiropractic of Tacoma PLLC, we specialize in treating injuries sustained in car accidents, and our team understands the physical and emotional toll these incidents can take on patients. From whiplash and soft tissue injuries to herniated discs and spinal misalignments, we offer comprehensive chiropractic care designed to address the root cause of your pain rather than simply masking symptoms. Our evidence-based treatment protocols are specifically structured to support your body's natural healing process, helping you recover faster and more effectively after a traumatic auto accident.

We are also proud to be a trusted provider for Labor & Industries (L&I) claims in Washington State, making us a go-to resource for workers who have suffered on-the-job injuries. Our experienced team is well-versed in navigating the L&I system and works diligently to ensure that every patient receives the benefits and medical attention they are entitled to. We handle the documentation and coordination with L&I adjusters so that you can focus entirely on getting better, reducing the administrative stress that often accompanies workplace injury claims.

For personal injury cases, Premier Chiropractic of Tacoma offers specialized care that aligns with the legal and medical documentation requirements needed to support your claim. We work closely with personal injury attorneys to provide thorough, accurate records that reflect the true extent of your injuries and the treatment required for recovery. Our goal is not only to restore your physical health but also to ensure you are fully supported throughout the legal process, giving you the best possible outcome in your case.

Our clinic features a welcoming, state-of-the-art environment where patients receive individualized treatment plans crafted by highly trained chiropractic professionals.
